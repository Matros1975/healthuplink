Privacy

Effective 24 November 2026

Frontside Solutions B.V. collects nothing through the Health Uplink app. The app sends your Apple Health data only to the server you paired it with, which you run and we do not.

What the app reads and sends

After you allow it in the Apple Health permission sheet, the app reads the types below and sends them to your receiver: daily values for your whole history, sleep nights for the last 365 days on the first sync (older nights when you use Resend from date), detailed records for the last 365 days, and the deletion of a record you delete in Apple Health. Each record carries its time, the time zone name it belongs to (such as Europe/Paris), its value and unit, its record ID (a random UUID that Apple Health gives it), the extra details Apple Health stores with it (for example the motion context of a heart rate reading), and the name, bundle identifier and model of the app or device that recorded it. Each batch also carries the app version and build, the iOS version, the iPhone model (such as iPhone16,1) and whether Low Power Mode was on. When you pair, the app also sends the device name iOS reports (usually just "iPhone"). All of this goes only to your own receiver.

The list is the same one the app shows before you agree. You choose which types to allow in the permission sheet; the app reads none that you turn off there.

Where it goes

Only to the receiver address in the QR code you scanned: a server you run, such as a home server or a cloud machine you rent. Frontside does not run that server and never receives the data. On the way, the connection is encrypted (Tailscale or HTTPS), and each batch is also encrypted to your receiver's key and signed by your iPhone. Security has the details.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • The outbox: encrypted batches waiting to be sent. The app cannot read them again; only your receiver can. Next to each batch it keeps the dates and type names it covers and how many records it holds, for the Sync activity screen.
  • The sync log: times, counts, error codes, batch numbers, type names and the start times of the periods the app read, without health values.
  • Keys: this iPhone's signing key, in the Keychain on this device only. It is not copied to iCloud or to other devices.
  • The pairing: the receiver's address and public key, and your home time zone.

The outbox and the sync log are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. To delete them, use Status > Pairing > Unpair and delete local data, or delete the app. iOS may keep the signing key after the app is deleted; it holds no health data, and unpairing first deletes it too.

Data on your server

What your receiver stores is yours, on a machine you control. You delete it there, for example with docker compose down -v, which removes the store, its backups and its keys. Unpairing in the app does not delete data already on the receiver.

Consent, and how to withdraw it

Before the first upload the app shows what it will send and where, and sends nothing until you agree. You can withdraw at any time:

  • turn off types or all access in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Health Uplink;
  • unpair in the app (Status > Pairing > Unpair and delete local data);
  • or delete the app.

AI tools you connect

When you connect an AI agent to your receiver, it reads what you ask it to read. If that agent runs on a hosted model, the company providing the model sees that data under its own terms. Frontside is not part of that exchange.

No tracking

The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporter of its own and no third-party code. It stores no health data in iCloud and sells or shares nothing.

What Frontside does process

  • Support e-mails you send us: your address and what you write. Mail to support@front-side-solutions.com is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to a Google Gmail mailbox, where we read and answer it. We use it only to answer you, and delete it once it is no longer needed for that, at the latest 12 months after your last message.
  • Crash reports and App Store statistics that Apple shares with developers only if you opted in to sharing them with app developers on your iPhone. They hold no health data. Apple keeps them under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

For these, the controller is Frontside Solutions B.V., the Netherlands (KvK 85332003). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you and in keeping the app working (GDPR article 6(1)(f)). Contact: support@front-side-solutions.com.

Recipients: Cloudflare, which forwards support e-mail, and Google, which hosts the mailbox, process it on our behalf. They may process it outside the EU, for example in the United States. For that they rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where that applies. We share support e-mails with no one else.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable form. Write to the address above. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

Health Uplink is not made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and the app sends nothing to us.

Consumer health data (Washington and similar laws)

This section is our consumer health data privacy policy under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar state laws, such as Nevada's and Connecticut's.

  • We collect no consumer health data. The app sends your health data only to the server you run, and none of it reaches Frontside.
  • We do not share or sell consumer health data, and we have none to share or sell.
  • If you put health information in a support e-mail yourself, we use it only to answer you. It passes only through Cloudflare, which forwards our mail, and Google, which hosts our mailbox, and we delete it once it is no longer needed, at the latest 12 months after your last message.
  • To use your rights under these laws (to know, access, delete, or withdraw consent), write to support@front-side-solutions.com. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date. If a change affects what the app sends or where, the app asks for your consent again before it sends anything under the new terms.
